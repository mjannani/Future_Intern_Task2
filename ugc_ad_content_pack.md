# Sample UGC Content Pack — DewDrop Hydrating Gel

> Fictional product concept. These are sample scripts, not real customer testimonials. Verify product details before publishing.

## Five Hook Options
1. “Want to simplify your skincare routine?”
2. “Here’s a simple way to talk about your daily skincare steps.”
3. “Building a skincare routine? Start by checking what your skin needs.”
4. “Let’s make your skincare routine easier to understand.”
5. “Before choosing a skincare product, here are three things to check.”

## Script 1 — Routine-Focused (15–20 seconds)
**Hook:** “Want to simplify your skincare routine?”
**Spoken script:** “When you’re choosing skincare, it helps to understand what each product is designed for. DewDrop Hydrating Gel is our sample product concept. Check its verified ingredients and directions, then decide whether it fits your routine.”
**On-screen text:** “Know the product • Check the ingredients • Follow directions”
**CTA:** “Learn More”
**Concept:** Educational product introduction.

## Script 2 — Product Information (20–30 seconds)
**Hook:** “Before adding a new product to your routine, check these details.”
**Spoken script:** “First, read the ingredient list. Next, follow the usage instructions. Finally, consider whether the product fits your personal preferences. For DewDrop Hydrating Gel, review the verified product details before deciding.”
**On-screen text:** “Ingredients | Directions | Product details”
**CTA:** “Explore Product Details”
**Concept:** Informational checklist.

## Script 3 — Problem-to-Decision (15–20 seconds)
**Hook:** “Too many skincare choices?”
**Spoken script:** “Choosing skincare can feel confusing when every product sounds different. Start with your own routine, read the product information carefully, and compare the details. If DewDrop Hydrating Gel matches what you’re looking for, learn more before you buy.”
**On-screen text:** “Understand your needs. Compare product details.”
**CTA:** “Learn More”
**Concept:** Helps viewers make an informed choice.
