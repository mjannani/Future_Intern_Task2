# Task 2 — Prompt Logic and Testing

## Prompt Logic
- Role: sets the AI's role as a UGC advertising copywriter.
- Objective: states the campaign goal.
- Product input: provides audience, platform, tone and verified facts.
- Script structure: ensures hook, context, product information and CTA are included.
- Rules: prevent fake testimonials and unsupported product claims.
- Output format: makes the scripts easy to compare and use.

## Test Plan
| Test | Variation | Check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Instagram Reels, 15 seconds | Hook and script fit short-form pacing |
| 2 | YouTube Shorts, 30 seconds | Script remains concise and platform-appropriate |
| 3 | No ingredients or product facts supplied | AI avoids inventing claims |
| 4 | Change product category to a local service | Tone and content adapt to the new input |

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Hook is clear and relevant
- [ ] Script sounds natural when read aloud
- [ ] CTA is specific
- [ ] Platform caption is appropriate
- [ ] No fake testimonials
- [ ] No invented ingredients, prices, discounts or results
- [ ] Claims checked against verified product information
- [ ] Actual test outcomes recorded
